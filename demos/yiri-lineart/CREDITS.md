All picture and sound in this film are generated by code in this folder. No images or recordings were downloaded.

- Paper texture `assets/textures/paper/Paper001_4K_color.jpg` (CC0, shipped with this repository); the collage also uses the repository's CC0 grain, dust and washi-tape textures.
- Music and sound effects: synthesised with `lib/audio` (mgaudio) and its CC0 VCSL instrument samples.
- Fonts (system fonts on the render machine, not bundled): WenQuanYi Zen Hei (GPL v2 with font embedding exception), Caladea (Apache 2.0), DejaVu Sans for the ☛ manicule glyph (Bitstream Vera licence).
- The documentary photograph (Dorothea Lange, 1936) and the 1960 car advertisement are referenced only as our own schematic silhouettes / contour lines; neither original image is reproduced, and no real figure is cut apart, warped or given facial detail.
